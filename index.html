Gerard Way

1977 – Present. Musician, Singer & Comic Book Writer/Artist

About Gerard

Gerard Arthur Way is an American singer, songwriter, and comic book writer who left a massive mark on both the alternative rock scene and the comic book industry. He is best known as the lead vocalist of My Chemical Romance and the creator of The Umbrella Academy.

Timeline

  1. 2001: Co-founded the band My Chemical Romance.
  2. 2004: Released Three Cheers for Sweet Revenge.
  3. 2006: Released The Black Parade.
  4. 2007: Published The Umbrella Academy.

Contributions

  • Lead singer and songwriter for My Chemical Romance.
  • Creator of The Umbrella Academy
  • Writer for the comic series Doom Patrol under DC Comics' Young Animal imprint.
  • Co-creator of the Marvel character Peni Parker (Spider-Verse).
